Clinical Work — 3D-Printed Skull for the Craniofacial Center

Surgical planning & patient education · data to a physical model

A project with the Craniofacial Center to produce a patient-specific 3D-printed skull and cross-section, for the patient and the surgical team to use in education and in preparing for surgery. Holding the actual anatomy, and seeing it in section, does something a scan on a screen cannot: it lets a surgical team rehearse and a patient understand what is ahead.

I ran the digital pipeline and built a scale-model prototype to confirm the whole process end to end, from imaging data to a clean, printable model. The final, full-detail model was then delivered to the Center to print on their high-resolution clinical machines.

Anatomy segmented from CT data; digital modeling, print preparation, and a proof prototype by me; final production on the Craniofacial Center's clinical printers.

Jaw Surgery Patient Manual — UIC Craniofacial Center

Patient education · surgical communication

A patient-education manual that explains corrective jaw surgery to patients and families: the types of jaw and bite problems, the anatomy involved, what each surgery does, the benefits and downsides, and how to prepare for and recover from the procedure. The challenge was taking a complex surgical and orthodontic subject and making it clear, reassuring, and usable for a non-medical reader facing a major decision. It pairs plain-language explanation with anatomical diagrams, and links to a patient-specific 3D surface model.
