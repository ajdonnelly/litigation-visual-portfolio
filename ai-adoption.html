AI Adoption & Assistance

Evaluating, piloting, and adopting generative AI inside a law firm

Getting a legal team to actually use new technology is a communication and change problem as much as a technical one. On my firm's AI committee, I help choose the right tools, put them into real workflows, and build the training that turns a pilot into everyday use.

From scattered experimentation to a structured pilot

I serve on my firm's AI committee, where I have conducted market research comparing legal-AI platforms, including Harvey, Legora, and Filevine, and assisted with vendor selection. We selected Filevine's LOIS and Console, in part because they were already integrated into our system of record. The firm moved to Filevine, and I have been part of the resulting 25-person firmwide pilot, with every user persona represented. Earlier, I was one of three participants in an enterprise pilot of Microsoft Copilot and Anthropic Claude.

Day to day, I work with operations, technology, attorneys, and the vendor's change-management team to gather requirements and build practice workflows, which the vendor configures on the back end, and then help integrate and fine-tune them in real use. I also help establish responsible-use practices, from confidentiality and protected health information to human verification of AI output.

Enablement, training & learning materials

Adoption depends on more than explaining what a tool can do; people need to understand how it fits into their work and why it is worth changing how they already do things. I design and deliver demonstrations, user guides, and e-learning built around real workflows and different levels of technical fluency, grounded in instructional design and adult-learning principles.

Selected enablement materials, a responsible-AI playbook, a workshop deck, a training module, and an adoption tracker, are available on request, and I am adding them here.

Where this goes

The same instinct behind a good demonstrative, understand the audience, find the right form, make it clear and defensible, is what makes AI adoption work in a professional setting. I want to keep building that bridge between how a firm practices and the tools that can help it.
